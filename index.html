Selected research

Multimodal perception and procedural video understanding.

01Accepted · 9th IC2IE 2026
Action RecognitionRGB–Depth

Procedural Video Understanding

Built a unified benchmark for early, intermediate, late, and hybrid RGB–Depth fusion using Video Swin Transformer/Swin3D and X3D under a consistent evaluation protocol.

  • Fine-grained assembly/disassembly action recognition
  • Accuracy–complexity analysis using latency, GFLOPs, and parameter count
  • Paper accepted for presentation at the 9th International Conference of Computer and Informatics Engineering (IC2IE 2026)
02Ongoing PhD research
GazeEgocentric Vision

Gaze-Aware Multimodal Recognition

Evaluated whether gaze improves action recognition through direct RGB+gaze input, explicit gaze markers, gaze-guided foveation, and feature-level modulation.

  • Best controlled configuration: approximately +1.5 percentage points over RGB
  • Controlled ablations documented negative, overfitting, and inconclusive variants
  • Targeted at human-aware perception for human-robot collaboration
03Dataset workflow
Project AriaTemporal Annotation

Multimodal Human-Participant Dataset

Designed and conducted an assembly/disassembly recording study using Meta Project Aria and two fixed-view cameras.

  • Approximately 24 participants with ethics/consent and data-quality monitoring
  • RGB, Depth, and gaze-oriented data preparation
  • Reusable temporal annotation and timestamp-mapping workflow
04Earlier work
EEGShared Control

Brain-Controlled Mobile Robot

Developed EEG-driven shared-control methods in MATLAB/Simulink, combining SVM intention classification with fuzzy and self-learning neuro-fuzzy safety control.

  • Human-participant EEG acquisition and classification
  • Intention tracking and obstacle-avoidance control
  • Simulation-based evaluation
